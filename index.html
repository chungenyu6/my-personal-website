Reliable AI that knows when to doubt.

I build metacognition-aware multimodal agentic AI systems that reason, verify, and know when they do not know—providing calibrated assurance in high-stakes settings where confident errors can be dangerous.

I’m a Ph.D. candidate in Intelligent Systems and Robotics at the University of West Florida and the Institute for Human & Machine Cognition. I work in the Artificial Super Intelligence Lab (ASI Lab) under the supervision of Prof. Brian Jalaian.
